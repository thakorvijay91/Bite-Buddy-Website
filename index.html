Frequently Asked Questions

How do l place an order?

Browse nearby restaurants, choose your favorite dishes, add them to your cart, and checkout in just a few taps.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support credit cards, debit cards, UPI, digital wallets, and cash on delivery in selected locations.

Can l track my delivery in real time?

Yes! You can track your order live from the restaurant kitchen all the way to your doorstep.

Is there a delivery fee?

We support credit cards, debit cards, UPI, digital wallets, and cash on delivery in selected locations.

How long does delivery usually take?

Most orders arrive within 20–40 minutes depending on your location, restaurant preparation time, and traffic conditions.

Can I cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, orders can be canceled before the restaurant starts preparing your food. Cancellation availability may vary by restaurant.
